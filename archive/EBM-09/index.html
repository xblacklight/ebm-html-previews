Statement of the firm

East Bay Mobility is a specialized traffic engineering consulting practice providing municipalities and legal professionals with independent, senior-level transportation engineering expertise. Led by a traffic engineer with 40 years of public- and private-sector experience, the firm focuses on assignments where professional judgment, practical municipal experience, and a thorough understanding of traffic engineering standards are more important than large consulting teams.

Rafat Raie, P.E. has spent more than forty years making California streets work, most of them on the public side of the counter. City Traffic Engineer for communities across Northern and Southern California, including Walnut Creek and San Rafael (Deputy Director of Public Works), where he led the first cloud-based emergency vehicle preemption system deployed in the United States, connecting 53 traffic signals with 56 police vehicles. Principal at Advanced Mobility Group; led the Contra Costa Transportation Authority’s first Digital Motions Master Plan. Since 2001 he has taught practicing engineers through UC Berkeley’s Technology Transfer Program (Fundamentals of Traffic Engineering, Traffic Signal Diagnostics, the California Traffic Engineer license review). B.S. Civil Engineering, San José State.
