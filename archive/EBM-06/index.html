GENERAL NOTE 1EAST BAY MOBILITY

East Bay Mobility is a specialized traffic engineering consulting practice providing municipalities and legal professionals with independent, senior-level transportation engineering expertise. Led by a traffic engineer with 40 years of public- and private-sector experience, the firm focuses on assignments where professional judgment, practical municipal experience, and a thorough understanding of traffic engineering standards are more important than large consulting teams.

01 · For Public Agencies

On-Call Senior Traffic Engineering & Development Review Services

For cities that need an experienced traffic engineer, not another full-service engineering firm.

S-01

Development Review

Peer review of traffic impact studies, VMT analyses, circulation plans, access studies, parking studies, conditions of approval, and development improvement plans.

S-02

Traffic Engineering Review

Signing and striping plans, traffic control plans, sight-distance evaluations, stop-control studies, traffic-safety evaluations, and operational reviews.

S-03

Municipal Staff Support

Serving as an extension of Public Works/Engineering staff when a city lacks sufficient traffic-engineering capacity or needs a senior independent opinion.

S-04

Special Assignments

Controversial developments, unusual access conditions, safety complaints, intersection evaluations, and second opinions on consultant recommendations.

03 · The Principal
FIELD REVIEWEAST BAY, CA

Rafat Raie, P.E.

Rafat Raie, P.E. has spent more than forty years making California streets work, most of them on the public side of the counter. City Traffic Engineer for communities across Northern and Southern California, including Walnut Creek and San Rafael (Deputy Director of Public Works), where he led the first cloud-based emergency vehicle preemption system deployed in the United States, connecting 53 traffic signals with 56 police vehicles. Principal at Advanced Mobility Group; led the Contra Costa Transportation Authority’s first Digital Motions Master Plan.
