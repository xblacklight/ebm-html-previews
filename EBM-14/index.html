Forensic · For legal professionals

Traffic Engineering Forensic & Litigation Support

A reconstructionist determines vehicle speeds, impact angles, braking, perception/reaction and vehicle dynamics. Our expertise answers a different question.

The core question

Did the roadway and traffic-control environment contribute to the collision, and was it designed, operated, maintained, or controlled consistent with accepted traffic-engineering practice?

Sixteen areas of traffic-engineering review
  1. Signing and pavement markings
  2. Intersection control
  3. Traffic signal operations
  4. Sight distance and visibility
  5. Roadway geometry
  6. Temporary traffic control / work zones
  7. Pedestrian and bicycle facilities
  8. Driveway/access design
  9. Speed management
  10. Traffic-control warrants
  11. Maintenance and changed field conditions
  12. Collision history and patterns
  13. MUTCD / California MUTCD compliance
  14. Design standards and engineering guidance
  15. Agency records and prior studies
  16. Whether an alleged roadway deficiency had a causal relationship to the collision

Leadership

Rafat Raie, P.E.

Rafat Raie, P.E. has spent more than forty years making California streets work, most of them on the public side of the counter.

He has served as City Traffic Engineer for communities across Northern and Southern California, including Walnut Creek and San Rafael, where he was also Deputy Director of Public Works and led the first cloud-based emergency vehicle preemption system deployed in the United States, connecting 53 traffic signals with 56 police vehicles. He went on to serve as a Principal at Advanced Mobility Group and led the Contra Costa Transportation Authority’s first Digital Motions Master Plan.

Since 2001 he has taught practicing engineers through UC Berkeley’s Technology Transfer Program, in Fundamentals of Traffic Engineering, Traffic Signal Diagnostics and the California Traffic Engineer license review. He holds a B.S. in Civil Engineering from San José State, and founded East Bay Mobility to bring that experience directly to the cities and counsel who need it.
